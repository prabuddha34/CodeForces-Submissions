A list consisting of <span class="tex-span"><i>n</i></span> last visited by the user pages and the inputted part <span class="tex-span"><i>s</i></span> are known. Your task is to complete <span class="tex-span"><i>s</i></span> to make it an address of one of the pages from the list.
